Problem solved

NCBA closes the gap between certificate lifecycle automation and real behavioral detection.

Machine identities such as APIs, microservices, containers, and IoT devices are now a critical attack surface. NCBA demonstrates short-lived credentials, behavior-based risk scoring, tiered response, and a verifiable local audit trail. Infrastructure integrations are simulated; no certificate authority, packet sensor, or blockchain is connected.
